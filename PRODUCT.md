# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite + Tailwind CSS + Lucide React (Fast, modular, client-side SPA with responsive mobile-first routing and high-performance GPU-friendly liquid glass & neumorphic styles)

## Users

1. **Students (Primary)**: College and university students managing grades, course loads, exam prep, study planning, peer study groups, and finding verified tutors or AI tutoring assistance.
2. **Tutors**: Academic subject-matter specialists listing offerings, setting availability and rates, hosting 1-on-1 or group video/chat sessions, and managing reviews and earnings.
3. **Administrators**: Platform operators moderating communities, verifying tutor credentials, managing courses and grading scales, and tracking subscription revenue and AI usage.

## Product Purpose

The Student Academic Platform is an all-in-one academic command center and digital learning ecosystem that unifies CGPA tracking, personalized study scheduling, exam simulation, tutor marketplace & live video sessions, peer study groups, and contextual AI tutoring into a single cohesive journey: "Measure → Plan → Learn → Connect → Practice → Improve".

## Positioning

Unlike isolated CGPA calculators, fragmented tutoring directories, or generic chat apps, this platform bridges academic diagnostics directly with action: a student's actual grades, weak test topics, and course deadlines automatically power their study schedule, tutor matching, and AI tutoring suggestions without leaving the platform.

## Operating Context

Students and tutors access the platform on mobile devices (smartphones at 320px–480px during commutes and lectures) and larger laptop/desktop screens (1024px–1920px in dorms, libraries, and study rooms). Display environments include high refresh rate 90Hz/120Hz screens requiring smooth GPU-friendly rendering.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Design Philosophy**: Zero Black (`#050505`) backdrop paired with Ghost Green (`#D7FFE0`) accents, tactile Neumorphic surfacing, and Liquid Glass floating interfaces with backdrop-filter blur.
- **Academic Management**: Multi-year, multi-semester configurable grading systems (4.0, 5.0, 7.0 scales), course units, GPA/CGPA computation, academic honors classification, and trend visualization.
- **Study Planner & Progress**: Goal setting, task deadlines, difficulty tracking, study streaks, visual progress metrics, and timeline/calendar views.
- **Exam / Test Prep**: Interactive timed tests across multiple question formats (multiple choice, true/false, short answer), real-time evaluation, and diagnostic breakdown into strong vs. weak topics.
- **Tutor Marketplace & Bookings**: Search/filtering by subject, pricing, and ratings; complete booking workflow with date/time pickers and session formats.
- **Real-time Video & Voice (ZEGOCLOUD-ready)**: Isolated service layer with simulated live call room, mic/camera controls, screen sharing, and participant chat.
- **Community**: Public and private study groups, interactive discussion feeds, resources, and live announcements.
- **AI Tutor**: Multi-mode assistant (Explain, Study, Quiz, Summary, Flashcard, Exam) contextualized by student course data.
- **Role-based Experience**: Dynamic role switching between Student, Tutor, and Admin to inspect full platform capabilities.

## Product Principles

1. **Complex Functionality Feels Simple**: Academic management can be overwhelming; the interface remains focused, calm, and uncluttered rather than enterprise-bloated.
2. **Action Follows Insight**: Every metric (like a lower GPA in CSC401 or a low test score in Algorithms) directly provides a one-click next step (study topic, ask AI tutor, or book a tutor).
3. **Tactile & Fluid Immersion**: Zero Black + Ghost Green with Neumorphism and Liquid Glass creates an unmistakable, premium, high-focus academic sanctum.
4. **Mobile-First Without Desktop Compromise**: Native ergonomics with bottom navigation on mobile, rich multi-column workspace and sidebar on desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Keyboard navigation and focus rings tailored to the Ghost Green design language.
- High contrast compliance on text layers over dark backgrounds.
- Respect for `prefers-reduced-motion` with graceful zero-lag fallbacks.
- Semantic HTML and ARIA labels across modal overlays, tab panels, and test interactions.
